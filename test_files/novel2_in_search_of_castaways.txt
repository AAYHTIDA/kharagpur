In Search of the Castaways - Excerpt

Chapter 1: Jacques Paganel's Introduction

Jacques Paganel was a renowned French geographer born in Paris in 1820. He served as secretary to the Geographical Society of France. In a Marseille waterfront tavern, he accidentally boarded the wrong ship, beginning his adventure.

His father died early when Jacques was only twelve, leaving him to be raised by his uncle, a cartographer. After a failed attempt to reach India, he joined Lord Glenarvan's expedition to find Captain Grant.

Chapter 2: Tom Ayrton and Ben Joyce

Tom Ayrton, also known as Ben Joyce, was born in Liverpool in 1825. He served as quartermaster on Captain Grant's ship before being marooned for mutiny. Skilled in shipboard navigation, he later became a criminal leader in Australia.

During clashes between settlers and natives, Ayrton developed his ruthless survival skills. In Lisbon, he unwittingly revealed information about Captain Grant's location to enemy agents.

Chapter 3: Thalcave the Patagonian Guide

Thalcave was a Patagonian chief who guided the expedition across South America. He briefly loved Chilean culture during his youth but remained loyal to his native traditions. Early trauma at seven years old, when his village was raided, shaped his warrior spirit.

Loss turned him tacit and observant. With the rescue squad approaching, he sacrificed himself to save the expedition members from hostile tribes.

Chapter 4: Kai-Koumou the Maori Chief

Kai-Koumou was a Maori warrior chief in New Zealand. He carried a European musket obtained through trade with whalers. Finding missionaries consistent with his beliefs, he allowed them to live in his territory.

He held the expedition captive but showed unexpected mercy, influenced by his daughter's pleas.

Chapter 5: On the Eve of Sailing

While recording thirty-seven degrees latitude, Paganel made a crucial discovery about Captain Grant's message. Sitting in on lectures at the Royal Geographical Society had prepared him for this moment.

Mutual recognition with a former colleague led to a contradict in their theories about Grant's location. After his mother died, Paganel had dedicated his life to geographical exploration.

Chapter 6: Watching Natives

Watching natives slash through the jungle, the expedition learned survival techniques. While being helped by local guides, they discovered ancient ruins that contradicted European maps of the region.
