Character Backstory: Eleanor Blackwood

Eleanor was born in December 1892 in Whitmore Bay, England, to Thomas Blackwood, a clockmaker, and Margaret, a former schoolteacher.

Growing up in her father's workshop at 14 Harbor Street, she developed an extraordinary ability to detect mechanical problems in clocks by sound alone. By age twelve, she had mastered skills that took most apprentices decades to learn.

She was colorblind, seeing only in shades of gray, but this never hindered her precision work. If anything, it enhanced her ability to focus on the mechanical aspects of her craft.

Eleanor never married, though she maintained a close friendship with Captain James Hartley, whom she met in London in 1916 during her work for the military. Their correspondence lasted over three decades.

She had a deep fear of water stemming from a near-drowning incident in childhood, and she never learned to swim despite living in a coastal town.

Eleanor preferred traditional communication methods and was known to avoid telephones entirely. She worked through many nights fueled by tea and shortbread, eventually training apprentices and ensuring women had equal opportunity to learn the craft.

She died in 1967 at age seventy-four, having never left England.
